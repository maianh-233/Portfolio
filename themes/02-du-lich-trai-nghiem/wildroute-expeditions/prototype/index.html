The journey

A sample three days on Fansipan via Tram Ton, the route our guides walk most often. Every expedition follows the same rhythm.

  1. Before you go

    Prepare

    A 30-minute video call with your lead guide, a fitness check and a packing list sent two weeks ahead. Rental boots and jackets are fitted in Sa Pa the evening before.

    • Gear fitting 7:00 PM
    • Safety briefing

  2. Day 1

    Depart

    Drive to Tram Ton pass at 1,900 m and start walking by 8:00 AM. Bamboo forest and old-growth rhododendron lead up to Camp 1, where the porters already have tea on.

    • 7.5 km
    • +300 m
    • Camp 1 at 2,200 m

  3. Day 2

    Explore

    The steepest day. Ladders and granite slabs up the ridge to Camp 2, then an afternoon free to photograph the cloud line before an early dinner.

    • 5 km
    • +600 m
    • Camp 2 at 2,800 m

  4. Day 3

    Summit and return

    Headlamps on at 4:00 AM for the last 343 m. Sunrise at 3,143 m, hot noodles at camp, then the long descent to Sa Pa for a well-earned dinner by 7:00 PM.

    • 11 km
    • Summit 3,143 m
    • Back in Sa Pa
