Where the village meets the sea

CASA SOL is built like a small coastal village. Whitewashed houses gather around shaded courtyards, stone steps lead down towards the water, and every doorway frames a piece of blue.

Architecture, garden and sea are never far apart. You wake to light on lime-washed walls, eat under the vines and end the day on a terrace that still holds the warmth of the afternoon.

The Beauty of Mediterranean Living

Lime-washed walls, local stone and hand-made terracotta. Materials that age gently and stay cool in August.

Deep arches and narrow windows soften the midday sun, so every room moves from bright to shaded through the day.

Wooden shutters, iron lanterns, steps worn smooth. The details are traditional; the comfort is quietly modern.
