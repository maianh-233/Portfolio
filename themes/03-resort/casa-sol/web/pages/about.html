About CASA SOL

A Place Shaped by the Mediterranean

CASA SOL began with a simple wish: to bring the warmth of a small coastal village into a place where guests can slow down and feel at home.

Hospitality, kept simple

We wanted a resort that feels less like a hotel and more like a village where you happen to be staying. Someone knows your name, the table by the window is kept for you, and lunch can wait until you are ready.

Beauty here is plain and honest: a whitewashed wall catching the evening light, bread still warm from the oven, the sound of the sea through an open shutter. Nothing is rushed, and nothing needs to be.

“Come for the sea. Stay for the pace.”

A line our hosts often repeat

Architecture and materials

Built with what the coast has always offered: lime, stone, clay, wood and a great deal of light.

White walls and arches

Lime wash reflects the heat and is renewed each spring. Arches give shade, frame the view and keep the rooms cool.

Natural stone and wood

Local stone for steps and garden walls, solid wood for doors, beams and shutters. Simple materials, made to be touched every day.

Natural light

Rooms are turned towards the morning sun and away from the hottest hours. Light moves across the walls like a slow clock.

Gardens and courtyards

Every house opens onto a courtyard: a tree for shade, pots of herbs, a bench to sit on after dinner.

The Mediterranean spirit

I

Warm Hospitality

Sincere welcome, never staged. Our hosts remember how you take your coffee and which path you like at sunset.

II

Local Flavours

Seasonal produce, local fishermen and growers, recipes passed from kitchen to kitchen along the coast.

III

Slow Living

Long breakfasts, afternoon rest, evenings without a plan. Time follows the light, not a schedule.

Arches, balconies and quiet corners

Every doorway frames a small piece of blue.
