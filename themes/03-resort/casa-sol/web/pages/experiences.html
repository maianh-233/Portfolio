Experiences & discovery

Savour Every Moment

Food from the sea and the garden, lanes of white houses, afternoons by the water. The Mediterranean is best enjoyed slowly.

Local food The village By the pool Gardens

Chapter 01 — Local flavours

A table in the open air

Meals follow the season and the morning catch: tomatoes still warm from the garden, grilled fish, wild greens, bread and olive oil from nearby groves.

Everything is served outdoors, under vines or beside the sea, and lunch is allowed to last all afternoon.

Chapter 02 — The coastal village

Postcards from the lanes

Leave the resort on foot and follow the stone lanes: white houses, painted doors, blossom over the walls and, at the end of every street, the sea.

Chapter 03 — Afternoons by the pool

Nowhere else to be

After lunch the resort grows quiet. A swim, a lounger in the shade, a novel you have been saving all year. The only sounds are water and cicadas.

Chapter 04 — Gardens & nature

Olive shade and stone paths

Old olive trees, bougainvillea on the walls, herbs along the paths. Walk early, when the air smells of rosemary, or late, when the stones still hold the warmth of the day.

Let the Days Unfold Slowly

A late breakfast, a walk through the village, a swim, a long dinner as the sky turns pink. At CASA SOL the best plan is to have none at all.
