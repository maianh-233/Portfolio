Rooms, villa & amenities

Rooms with a Mediterranean Soul

Lime-washed walls, linen, local stone and windows that frame the sea or the garden. Every room is shaped by the landscape around it.

01 Coastal Suite 02 Courtyard Room 03 Private Villa

No. 01

Coastal Suite

Wide windows turned towards the horizon, pale linen and walls the colour of chalk. The room changes with the light, from soft blue mornings to golden evenings on the balcony.

  • Private balcony facing the sea
  • King bed dressed in washed linen
  • Stone bathroom with rain shower
  • Reading corner by the window

No. 02

Courtyard Room

Arched doors open straight onto a quiet courtyard of potted lemon trees and climbing jasmine. Cool terracotta underfoot, thick walls and a sense of privacy that feels like a home in the old village.

Garden terrace Vaulted ceiling Terracotta floors

No. 03

Private Villa

Terrace · Plunge pool · Sea view

A house of its own, set a little apart from the village. Two bedrooms, a living room that opens fully to the terrace, a plunge pool and a long table for dinners outdoors. Space to spread out, and complete quiet.

  • Two bedrooms
  • Private plunge pool
  • Outdoor dining terrace
  • Shaded day beds

Around the resort

Places to spend the day

Outdoor pool

A long pool framed by olive trees and loungers, open from sunrise until the first stars.

Mediterranean garden

Olive trees, rosemary, lavender and bougainvillea, with stone paths and benches hidden in the shade.

Outdoor restaurant

Breakfast, long lunches and candlelit dinners on a stone terrace a few steps from the water.

Lounge & terraces

White terraces with cushions and low tables, made for reading, an afternoon drink or simply watching the boats.
