About SANDLINE

A resort that behaves like a landscape.

SANDLINE sits low on a dry ridge in the Tabernas desert. Its walls are poured from the same ochre earth that surrounds them, so the buildings read as terraces of rock rather than objects placed on top.

Rooms open towards the east for first light and close to the west against the afternoon heat. Every courtyard, opening and pool follows the path of the sun.
