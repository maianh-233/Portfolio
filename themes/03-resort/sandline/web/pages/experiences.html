Days measured by light.

From the first line of sun on the dunes to the last star overhead, each day here follows the desert's own rhythm.

01

Night sky, uninterrupted

With no town lights for thirty kilometres, the Milky Way rises clearly over the ridge. Our astronomer leads sessions on the roof terrace with a 14-inch telescope, blankets and hot almond milk.

Nightly / 90 minutes

02

Into the canyons

Guided walks through the dry ravines of the Rambla de Tabernas, where layers of rock record millions of years. Routes range from a gentle hour to a full day with lunch in the shade of a cliff.

Daily at sunrise / 2 to 7 hours

03

Dinner where the road ends.

Once a week the kitchen moves out to a long table on the dunes. Lamb slow-cooked in clay, almonds, red prawns from Garrucha and wine from the hills of Laujar, served as the sky turns from orange to violet.

Thursdays, April to October. Twelve guests at most.

04

Slow hours by the water

The hottest part of the day is for doing very little. Shaded loungers along the reflecting pool, cold mint tea, and a massage in the open pavilion when the breeze picks up.

Every afternoon / 13:00 to 17:00

05

The makers of Almería

Half-day visits with the people who keep the region's crafts alive, arranged in small groups and always with time to talk.

  1. a.

    Pottery in Níjar

    Throw a bowl with a family of potters working the same clay for four generations.

  2. b.

    Esparto weaving

    Learn to braid the wild grass of the hills into baskets and mats.

  3. c.

    An old olive mill

    Taste the new season's oil at a stone mill in the Alpujarra foothills.
