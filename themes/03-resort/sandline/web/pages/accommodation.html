Rooms that open to the horizon.

Three residence types, each drawn around a different relationship between shelter and landscape.

01

Desert Suite

One long room beneath a thirty-centimetre concrete roof. The roof overhangs the glass by two metres, so the suite stays shaded at midday while the terrace catches the morning sun.

The space

  • 68 m² interior
  • Shaded east terrace
  • Sleeps two

In the suite

  • Plunge pool
  • Stone soaking tub
  • Oak reading bench

02

Courtyard Villa

Four rooms turn inward around a walled garden of white gravel, agave and a single olive tree. Sliding glass walls fold away, so the courtyard becomes the true living room from April to October.

The space

  • 140 m² interior
  • Private courtyard
  • Sleeps four

In the villa

  • Outdoor shower
  • Fire bowl
  • Two bedrooms

03

Horizon Residence

Set on the highest point of the ridge, the residence is a single forty-metre wall of glass held between two stone ends. Bedrooms, kitchen and living space all share the same uninterrupted view west to the Sierra Alhamilla.

A sunken terrace and private pool sit below the line of sight, so the horizon is never broken.

The space

  • 260 m² interior
  • Three bedrooms
  • Sleeps six

In the residence

  • Private pool
  • Chef's kitchen
  • Sunken terrace

Shared spaces, shaped for stillness.

01

Reflecting Pool

Sixty metres of still water, heated in winter, dark-lined to mirror the sky.

02

Restaurant

Breakfast until noon, dinner from eight. Produce from farms in the Andarax valley.

03

Library Lounge

Books on architecture and the desert, tea all day, no screens.

04

Thermal Baths

Sauna, warm stone room and a cold pool cut into the hillside.

Before you arrive

Rates and availability are shared on request by our reservations team.

Check-in from 3 pm, check-out until noon. Late departures can be arranged when the residence is free.
Almería airport is 35 minutes by car, Málaga about two hours. We can arrange transfers from either.
Families are welcome in Courtyard Villas and Horizon Residences. Desert Suites are designed for two adults.
Two Desert Suites have step-free access and a level shower. Paths between buildings are ramped throughout.

Architecture shaped by the land, and the light that crosses it.
