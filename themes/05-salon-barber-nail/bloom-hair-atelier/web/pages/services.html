Services

Made for the way you wear your hair

Every appointment starts with a consultation. We only suggest what your hair genuinely needs.

Four steps to every visit

  1. 01

    Discover

    You share your routine, reference photos and past treatments. We look at texture and fibre health.

  2. 02

    Consult

    Your stylist proposes the shape, shade, timing and exact cost before anything starts.

  3. 03

    Create

    Cutting, color mixing and texture work with bond protection, checking tone at every stage.

  4. 04

    Finish

    A finishing blow-dry, home care advice and a suggested date for your next refresh.
