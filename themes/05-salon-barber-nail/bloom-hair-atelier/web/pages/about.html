Inside BLOOM

A calm, daylight-filled atelier where color is mixed slowly and for one person at a time.

Our Story

BLOOM means to open into flower. We chose the name because we believe beauty is not manufactured in a salon but drawn out: everyone already carries a radiant version of themselves that just needs the right shade and shape to open.

ATELIER reminds us to work like a craft studio: fewer guests each day, more time for every head of hair, and no single formula for everyone.

The BLOOM team

Our Philosophy

No. 01

Beauty

Beauty is hair that suits your face, skin and pace of life, not hair that copies a reference photo.

No. 02

Creativity

We mix color the way painters mix paint: testing, adjusting and suggesting shades you may never have considered.

No. 03

Individuality

No two heads of hair are alike. Each visit is its own design, recorded so the next one picks up where it left off.

Color Director

Vu Mai Anh

Mai Anh oversees every color formula at BLOOM. She swatch-tests a small strand before each new color and logs every formula so your next appointment matches the last.

Specialties
Balayage, lavender brown, color correction
Signature service
Balayage

Cut & Shape

Nguyen Phuong Thao

Thao believes a good cut should still look good after a rushed blow-dry. She dry-cuts section by section, watching how hair falls before shaping each layer.

Specialties
Layers, bobs, curtain fringes
Signature service
Soft Layer Cut

Texture & Styling

Le Gia Han

Han chooses the gentlest perm solution each hair type allows and always tests elasticity first. She also styles hair for events and shoots at the salon.

Specialties
Soft waves, root perms, event updos
Signature service
Soft Wave Perm
