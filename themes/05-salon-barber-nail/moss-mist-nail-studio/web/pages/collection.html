Botanical Collection

Inspired by Nature

Thirteen designs gathered from gardens, forest walks and the flower market. Pick one as it is, or ask us to adapt it to your nails.

The designs

Morning Daisy

Cream, petal white, soft yellow

A clean French tip with one hand-painted daisy on the ring finger.

Tortoise Bark

Amber, espresso, honey

Layered translucent gel that reads like polished bark or old horn.

Lagoon Fern

Teal, sage, mist

Cool water greens, alternating nail by nail like ferns at a pond edge.

Linen White

Milk, linen, bare

A sheer milky finish that looks like washed linen in daylight.

Ginkgo Autumn

Saffron, rust, oat

Our October set: warm yellows with a single fan-shaped ginkgo leaf.

Marigold Market

Apricot, nude, gold

Inspired by the marigold stalls at Quang Ba flower market at dawn.

Woven Reed

Oat, sand, raw cane

Soft neutrals that sit well with natural fibres, rattan and wood.

Peony Hush

Blush, rose, cream

Layered pinks with a pressed petal on two nails.

Fallen Leaf

Tan, caramel, ivory

Fine leaf veins drawn in caramel over a sheer ivory base.

Bare Blush

Nude pink, sheer gloss

Your nails, slightly better. The most booked design in the studio.

River Clay

Terracotta, rust, stone

A warm clay colour that deepens in sunlight, finished matte or gloss.

Mustard Fog

Mustard, grey mist

For Hanoi's misty late autumn mornings, alternating nail by nail.

Frost Heather

Mauve, white, silver

Our winter set: heather mauve with a light frost of silver on the tips.

Where the designs begin

Each collection starts on a walk. We photograph what is growing, press a few leaves, and test colours side by side with the real thing before a design reaches your nails.

West Lake mornings

Lotus leaves, reeds and the soft grey water behind the studio.

The flower market

Marigolds, peonies and daisies, bought before sunrise at Quang Ba.

Four seasons

A new seasonal set every three months, gone when the season ends.
