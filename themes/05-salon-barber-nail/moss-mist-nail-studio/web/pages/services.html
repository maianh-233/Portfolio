Caring for your nails at home

A few small habits keep colour fresh and nails strong between visits.

A drop of cuticle oil each evening keeps the skin soft and helps gel stay flexible. We send you home with a small bottle of our camellia blend.
Hot water and cleaning products are the main reason polish lifts early. Gloves for dishes and gardening make a real difference.
Avoid opening cans or peeling labels with your nails. Pressed flower art in particular lasts longer when the tips are not knocked.
Peeling takes layers of nail with it. If a nail lifts, file the edge lightly and come in for a gentle soak-off removal.
After two or three gel sets in a row, a week of bare nails with a strengthening treatment helps them recover.
