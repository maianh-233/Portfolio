Photo: potted plants between a window and a shelf in natural light.

How a balcony garden became a nail studio

Thao and Mai met while training as nail technicians in 2019. Both found the salons they worked in loud, bright and rushed, and both spent their days off in Mai's balcony garden, pressing leaves into notebooks.

MOSS & MIST began as a weekend experiment: manicures on that balcony, with designs taken from whatever was growing. Friends brought friends. In 2023 we moved into a ground-floor space near West Lake with room for six tables and many more plants.

We still work the same way. One guest per artist, no rushing, and nature as the reference for every colour and line.

What a visit feels like

  1. Arrive and slow down

    A cup of lemongrass tea and a warm towel while we talk through what you would like.

  2. Choose together

    Swatch sticks and pressed samples on a stone tray, held against your skin in daylight.

  3. Unhurried care

    One artist, one guest, soft music and the time each step actually needs.

  4. Leave with a ritual

    A small bottle of cuticle oil and a care card so your set lasts until next time.
