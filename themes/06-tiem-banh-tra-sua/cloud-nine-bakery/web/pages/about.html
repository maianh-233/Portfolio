Our story

A small bakery built from afternoons spent watching clouds and one very old oven.

Inspired by the sky

Cloud Nine began on a small balcony in Da Lat, where Linh baked her first meringues and let them cool under a sky full of clouds. She realised she wanted to make cakes that made people feel lighter, like lying in the grass and watching the sky drift by.

We learned from Japanese baking: less sugar, more air, a texture as soft as cotton. Then we added Vietnamese fruit by the season: Da Lat strawberries, Hoa Loc mangoes, Lam Dong passion fruit.

“A good cake should be light enough that you want one more bite.”

Every cake here is made by hand by a team of five bakers. We use no artificial colours: the pinks come from strawberry and beetroot, the lilacs from taro and butterfly pea flower.

Inside the little shop

A room above the clouds

Cream walls, voile curtains and a ceiling painted the pale blue of a morning sky. The east windows fill the room with light.

28 seats, a window bar and a tiny balcony full of hydrangeas.

Good for afternoon tea dates, quiet work or a book on your own.

A day in the clouds

  2. 08:00

    Doors open and the cakes and still-warm pastries go on the counter.

  3. 15:00

    The afternoon bake

    Fresh mousse and cream puffs arrive for afternoon tea.

  4. 21:30

    Lights out

    Anything left is given to a shelter near the shop every evening.
