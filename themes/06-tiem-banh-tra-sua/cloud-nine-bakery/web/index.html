A note from our kitchen

Baked slowly, served softly

We opened Cloud Nine for a simple reason: one good bite can make a whole afternoon feel lighter. Every layer of cream is whipped by hand, every meringue dried slowly at a low heat.

Our cakes are low in sugar, rich in seasonal fruit, and always paired with a warm or iced milk tea.

Linh and the Cloud Nine kitchen
