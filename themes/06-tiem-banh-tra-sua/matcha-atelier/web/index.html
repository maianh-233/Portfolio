Four things we never rush

  1. 01

    Uji matcha, ground on stone

    Tencha leaves are shaded for 21 days before harvest, then ground slowly at 40 grams an hour to keep the aroma.

  2. 02

    Whisked when you order

    Nothing is batched in the morning. Each cup starts with 2 grams of powder and a clean chasen.

  3. 03

    Baked every morning

    Mochi, rolls and crêpes are made from 5 am, with less sugar so the tea leads.

  4. 04

    Served on handmade ceramics

    Hand-thrown bowls from Bat Trang and Karatsu. No two are alike.

“Tea is a work of art and needs a master hand to bring out its noblest qualities.”

Kakuzō Okakura, The Book of Tea, 1906
