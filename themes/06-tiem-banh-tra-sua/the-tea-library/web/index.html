The Art of Tea

We believe a good pot of tea begins with patience. Water is heated to the right degree for each leaf, every pot is timed with an hourglass and poured into a warmed porcelain cup.

No hurry and no tea bags. Only leaves, water and enough time for the flavour to tell its own story.

Read about tea culture

Four steps to a proper pot

  1. 01

    Warm the pot

    Rinse pot and cups with hot water so the tea holds its heat.

  2. 02

    Measure the leaf

    Three grams for every 200 ml, weighed for each pot rather than guessed.

  3. 03

    Mind the water

    80°C for green tea, 95°C for black, a rolling boil for herbal blends.

  4. 04

    Wait for the sand

    Every pot arrives with an hourglass. When the sand runs out, your tea is ready.
