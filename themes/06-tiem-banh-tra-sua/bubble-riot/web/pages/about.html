Loud by
accident.

BUBBLE RIOT is a milk tea counter with a bakery problem. Orange walls, one speaker, and a queue that started as a joke.

See the board Find the shop
Est. 2019

The first corner, Trang Tien alley.

One orange corner.

Trang Tien · 12 m² · 3 staff

We opened in 2019 with a rented cooler, one speaker and a recipe written on the back of a receipt. The plan was to sell enough cups to pay the rent and stop there. The queue had other ideas.

In 2021 we bought an oven because the queue kept asking for something to chew. The bakery took over the back half of the shop and the playlist got louder. Nothing about it was strategic. It just happened, and people kept coming back.

Today we still brew in small batches, still laminate croissants by hand, and still argue about sugar levels at the counter. The orange wall is the only thing we agreed on immediately.

“We are not trying to be everywhere. Just this corner, done properly.”

Mai & Duc · co-founders

Three rules we do not bend

Written on the wall behind the counter, in marker, after a long argument.

01

Loud

Strong tea, honest sugar, a speaker at a volume the neighbours have opinions about. If a cup tastes shy, it does not leave the counter.

02

Honest

Tea from Thai Nguyen, milk with a date on it, fruit cut the same morning. Nothing is hidden behind a flavour syrup we cannot pronounce.

03

Local

One shop, one street, one team. We buy from the market two blocks away and we still know the names of the people who queue at 4pm.
