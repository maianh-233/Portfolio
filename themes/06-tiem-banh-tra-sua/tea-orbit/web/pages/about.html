Our story

A pastry wrapper, then a drink.

The first sketch was drawn on the back of a pastry wrapper: one cup, a few stacked layers of color and small dots standing in for tapioca. That is where the name TEA ORBIT came from — everything in the shop revolves around the tea, from milk, fruit and cream to the pastry on the side.

That is why our menu has only four families. Tea is the base. Milk, fruit and cream are the middle layers. Toppings sit at the bottom so the last sip is still fun. Every cup can be read from the outside in, and drunk from the bottom up.

“We don't add flavor to hide the tea. We build layers so the tea can be seen.”

The rest is experimentation. A notebook in the kitchen logs every batch: water temperature, steep time, milk ratio, ice. Some batches went down the sink because the tea turned bitter; others were too sweet to rescue. A recipe earns its place around the fortieth trial, when nobody at the counter has to ask, “what is this missing?”

Tran Khanh Linh

Founder & flavor lead

Brand values

Three things we keep.

Not a slogan for the wall. These are the three questions we ask again every week, when a new batch reaches the counter.

01

Creativity

Every recipe starts with a tasting, not a trend. The new comes from how things are combined, not from added flavoring.

02

Balanced flavor

Temperature, texture and ratio matter as much as aroma — sometimes more. A balanced cup is just as good in the first, middle and last sip.

03

A modern experience

Sometimes it is just the pour, how the cup is handed over, the name on the menu. A modern experience lives in small details, repeated every day.
