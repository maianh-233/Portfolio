Three layers per cup

Flavor in Motion

Every cup has three layers. Pick one to see how we put them together.

Cold brewed 12 hours, hot brewed 6 minutes

Assam black tea and Taiwanese oolong are brewed fresh every morning. Tea is the base layer, so its light bitterness must still come through after the milk.

Fresh milk, oat milk or cheese cream

Milk goes in last and slowly, so it swirls between the tea and the ice. You choose the sweetness: 30, 50 or 70%.

Pearls cooked every 3 hours

Brown sugar pearls, butterfly pea pudding and sea salt cream. Toppings sit at the bottom, so the last sip is still fun.

How we create
“We don’t add flavor to impress. We build layers so the tea can be seen.”

The novelty comes from layering, temperature and ratio, and the tea always stays at the center. Same with our pastries: less sweet, baked the same day, made to go with tea.

Tran Khanh Linh, founder and flavor lead
