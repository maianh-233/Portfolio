Our Story

A wooden table, one jar of starter, and a habit of baking more than two people could eat.

Bread, the slow way

BLOOM & CRUMB began at a kitchen table, with one jar of starter and a habit of baking more than we needed. Friends asked for loaves, then the neighbours did, and the kitchen table slowly became a counter.

Our idea of a bakery is simple: good flour, water, salt, time, and hands that know when to leave the dough alone. We bake what the season allows, and stop when it is gone.

“We would rather sell out early than skip a step.”

Today the bakery is a small room with a wood-topped counter, a deck oven and a window full of morning light. The recipes are still short. The dough still takes its time.

Ingredients, process and the seasons

Ingredients

We choose flour by how it behaves: how much water it drinks, how it stretches, how it tastes after a long ferment. Each new batch is tried in a small loaf before it reaches the counter.

Process

A starter fed twice a day, dough folded by hand, a long rest in the cool overnight. Time builds a flavour that no shortcut can.

Seasonal inspiration

Strawberries in spring, peaches in summer, pumpkin when the air turns cool. The tart menu changes when the fruit does.
