Baked Slowly, Made with Nature

Long-fermented loaves, butter-laminated pastry and fruit tarts, shaped by hand each morning.

See the menu Plan a visit

We let time do most of the work.

Our dough rests for up to 36 hours before it meets the oven. That slow rise builds a deeper flavour, a crackling crust and a crumb that stays soft for days.

We bake in small batches, follow what each season brings, and keep our recipes short enough to read aloud.

From Our Oven

Three bakes our regulars ask for first, and what goes into each one.

Country Sourdough

Stone-ground wheat, rye starter and sea salt. A 36-hour ferment for a gentle tang and a crackling crust.

Butter Croissant

Cultured butter, laminated over three days, finished with a thin honey glaze.

Summer Fruit Tart

Almond cream, vanilla custard, kiwi, peach and raspberry on a butter crust.

Inspired by Nature

A walk through the morning market decides the week's tarts. Flowers on the counter, wheat on the shelf, fruit at its ripest: the bakery follows the garden, not the other way around.

Flowers
