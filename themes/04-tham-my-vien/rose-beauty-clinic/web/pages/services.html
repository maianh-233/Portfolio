Questions & Clarity

Everything you might wonder before your first visit

We believe that calm confidence is the foundation of skin healing. If you have any further questions, our doctors are always happy to discuss them during your analysis.

Or call us directly at 028 3930 4718

Completely complimentary. Your 45-minute appointment includes multi-spectral skin scanning, lipid barrier assessment, and a private 1:1 conversation with our dermatologist. There is never any obligation or pressure to purchase a treatment or product.

You do not need to diagnose yourself beforehand. During your consultation, our doctor assesses your skin's biological reactivity, prior treatment history, and daily routine to design a personalized pathway tailored specifically to you.

ROSÉ specializes in gentle, low-invasive protocols that work in harmony with your body. Virtually all our treatments have zero downtime; your skin feels calm and glowing right after the appointment, allowing you to resume normal work and social routines immediately.

Every session comes with 14 days of dedicated follow-up. Your personal therapist checks in with you on day 2, day 7, and day 14 to monitor progress, answer questions, and adjust your home skincare routine as your skin adapts.
