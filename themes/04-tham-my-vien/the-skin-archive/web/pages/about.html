Why an archive?

THE SKIN ARCHIVE began with a familiar problem: clients arriving with drawers of half-used products and no memory of what had really helped.

We decided to treat every skin as a subject worth documenting. Not with clinical coldness, but with the patience of someone keeping a careful notebook.

“We would rather do less, and know why.”

Each client keeps a care file with us: what we observed, what we did and how the skin responded. It lets us plan calmly and explain every choice.

That file belongs to you. You can look through it at any visit, and it is used only for your own care.

The consultation, step by step

About 45 minutes. Nothing is done to your skin unless you agree.

  1. 00:00

    Welcome and intake notes

    A short form on your routine, current products and any past reactions.

  2. 00:05

    Conversation

    What bothers you, since when, and what you hope for.

  3. 00:20

    Skin observation

    A light cleanse, then a look in proper light at texture, moisture, oil and sensitivity.

  4. 00:35

    Your first page of notes

    We read the notes together and suggest a starting point, with reference prices.

  5. 00:45

    You decide

    Book now, think it over, or simply take the home routine with you.
