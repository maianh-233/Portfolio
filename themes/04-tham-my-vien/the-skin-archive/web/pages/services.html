Services & Price Guide

Six service groups, filed like six folders. Each entry lists its purpose, what to know and a reference price.

Prices are for reference. Your exact plan and cost are confirmed after the consultation.

SA-01

Skin Reading

Where every file begins. We listen, look and write down how your skin is before suggesting any treatment.

Purpose

Understand your skin today and choose the right place to start.

Good to know

Come with a bare face if you can, and bring the products you currently use.

Purpose

See what is working and what needs to change.

Good to know

Usually booked 4 to 6 weeks after your previous visit.

SA-02

Cleansing & Barrier

For skin that feels congested, tight, or weakened after the wrong products.

Purpose

Clear congestion and let the skin breathe.

Good to know

Skin may look slightly pink for a few hours afterwards.

Purpose

Reduce stinging and tightness so skin tolerates products again.

Good to know

Pause acid or retinoid products for a few days before your visit.

SA-03

Hydration

For skin that looks dull, flakes a little, or feels tight from air-conditioning and sun.

Purpose

Restore moisture and leave skin softer and smoother.

Good to know

Suits most skin types; you can wear makeup straight after.

Purpose

Keep moisture steady rather than for a few days only.

Good to know

Appointments can be moved with 24 hours notice.

SA-04

Blemish & Sensitive Care

Slow and gentle. Where a concern needs medical treatment, we will advise you to see a dermatologist.

Purpose

Reduce congestion and avoid marks left by picking.

Good to know

Tell us if you are using any prescription acne medication.

Purpose

Ease burning and redness and help skin settle.

Good to know

We patch-test products on a small area before using them on your face.

SA-05

Renewal & Texture

For uneven tone, rough texture or visible pores. We always start at a gentle level.

Purpose

Smoother surface and a more even tone over time.

Good to know

Careful sun protection is needed for at least a week afterwards.

Purpose

Gradual, controlled progress rather than one strong session.

Good to know

Not done on sunburnt skin or open wounds.

SA-06

Body Skin Care

The same documented approach, applied to back, shoulders and arms.

Purpose

Ease congestion in an area that is hard to reach yourself.

Good to know

Wear a loose, comfortable top.

Purpose

Soften dry, rough areas.

Good to know

Avoid hot showers right after the session.
