Issue No. 14 About us

A diary, not a clinic.

How a dermatologist and two aestheticians built a quieter way to look after skin in the middle of District 1.

Dear reader,

I opened SKIN DIARY after twelve years in hospital dermatology, where I saw the same story again and again: good skin damaged by too many products and treatments chosen in a hurry.

So we built the opposite. Fewer treatments, done slowly. A written record for every client, updated after each visit, so that nothing is guessed. Rooms that feel more like a reading corner than a clinic.

We do not sell products and we do not chase trends. We read your skin, we tell you honestly what it needs, and we write it down.

Nguyen Minh Chau

Founder and dermatologist

How a first visit works

  1. 01

    Consult

    Thirty minutes about your routine, history and what you want your skin to feel like.

  2. 02

    Read

    A magnified skin analysis and standard photos that open your personal skin record.

  3. 03

    Plan

    A written plan with treatments, timing and a clear price before anything begins.

  4. 04

    Review

    A follow-up after two weeks to note how your skin responded and adjust the next step.
