Ingredients, 4 min read

How to layer a serum without overloading your skin

Serums are concentrated, which is exactly why more is rarely better. Start with clean, slightly damp skin and apply the thinnest texture first: water-light serums before gels, gels before creams.

Introduce one active at a time. If you add vitamin C and a retinoid in the same week and your skin stings, you will not know which one caused it. Give each new product seven to ten days before deciding whether it works for you.

Two or three drops cover the whole face. Press, do not rub, and wait a minute before your moisturiser so each layer can settle.

Written by the SKIN DIARY treatment team.

Routine, 3 min read

The four-step evening routine we give most new clients

  1. Cleanse twice if you wore sunscreen. An oil or balm first, then a gentle gel cleanser.
  2. Hydrate. A simple toner or essence on damp skin, patted in with the hands.
  3. Treat. The one active your skin record says you need, nothing more.
  4. Seal. A moisturiser matched to the season, lighter in the dry months of the year than you might think.

Bring your current products to your consultation and we will edit the routine with you.

Seasons, 3 min read

Rainy season skin: why oily can still mean dehydrated

Humid months make skin look shiny, so many people strip it with harsh cleansers and skip moisturiser. The result is often more oil, not less, because dehydrated skin tries to protect itself.

Swap foaming cleansers for a low-pH gel, keep a light hydrating layer, and do not drop sunscreen on grey days. Clouds block far less UV than they seem to.

Written by the SKIN DIARY treatment team.
