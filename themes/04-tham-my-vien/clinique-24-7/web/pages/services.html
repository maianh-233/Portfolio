01

Skin treatment

Medical treatment of common skin conditions, planned after an assessment.

ServicePurposeGood to knowReference price

  1. S.01

    Acne treatment programme

    Doctor-led plan combining in-clinic extraction, topical therapy and follow-up visits.

    Purpose

    Reduce active breakouts and prevent new scarring.

    Good to know

    Results build over several weeks. Bring a list of products you use.

    Reference price

  2. S.02

    Acne scar resurfacing

    Fractional laser or microneedling to remodel depressed scars, chosen after assessment.

    Purpose

    Soften the depth and edges of established scars.

    Good to know

    Expect redness for a few days. Active acne is treated first.

    Reference price

    from 2,500,000 ₫

  3. S.03

    Pigmentation and melasma care

    Assessment of pigment type, then a combined plan of topicals, peels or low-energy laser.

    Purpose

    Even out dark patches and reduce recurrence.

    Good to know

    Daily sun protection is part of the treatment, not an extra.

    Reference price

    from 1,200,000 ₫

    per session

  4. S.04

    Redness and pore refinement

    Light-based treatment for visible vessels and enlarged pores, with barrier support.

    Purpose

    Calm persistent redness and refine skin texture.

    Good to know

    Usually little downtime. Avoid tanning before sessions.

    Reference price

    from 1,500,000 ₫

    per session

02

Intensive skin care

Clinical care that keeps the skin barrier healthy between and after treatments.

  1. S.05

    Medical facial cleansing

    Deep cleansing, gentle extraction and a calming mask, adapted to your skin type.

    Purpose
    Clear congestion and support oily or acne-prone skin.
    Good to know
    No retinoids for 3 days before the session.

    from 550,000 ₫ per session

  2. S.06

    Chemical peel

    Controlled exfoliation with an acid chosen and timed by the doctor.

    Purpose
    Improve dullness, fine texture and mild pigmentation.
    Good to know
    Light flaking for a few days. Strict sun protection after.

    from 900,000 ₫ per session

  3. S.07

    Barrier repair programme

    Hydration infusion and a simplified home routine for sensitised skin.

    Purpose
    Reduce stinging, tightness and reactive redness.
    Good to know
    Often recommended before laser or peel treatments.

    from 800,000 ₫ per session

03

Non-surgical aesthetics

Procedures performed by doctors only, without incisions or general anaesthesia.

  1. S.08

    Anti-wrinkle injections

    Neuromodulator injections for expression lines of the forehead, frown and eye area.

    Purpose
    Soften dynamic lines while keeping natural expression.
    Good to know
    Effect appears over about two weeks and is temporary.

    from 3,000,000 ₫

    per area

  2. S.09

    Skin booster injections

    Micro-injections of hyaluronic acid to improve hydration and skin quality.

    Purpose
    Improve hydration, fine lines and skin elasticity.
    Good to know
    Small bumps settle within a day or two.

    from 4,500,000 ₫

    per session

  3. S.10

    Energy-based skin tightening

    Radiofrequency or focused ultrasound applied to the deeper layers of the skin.

    Purpose
    Support firmness of the jawline, cheeks and neck.
    Good to know
    Changes develop gradually over the following months.

    from 8,000,000 ₫

    per session
