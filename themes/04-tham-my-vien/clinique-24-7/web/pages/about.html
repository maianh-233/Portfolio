A skin clinic that works like a medical practice.

Who we are, who treats you, where you are treated and the standards behind each visit.

The clinic

CLINIQUE 24/7 is a medical aesthetics and skin care clinic. We were set up for people who want visible results from treatments that are explained, documented and supervised by doctors.

We do not sell packages at the front desk. Every course of treatment follows a skin assessment and a written plan, and every price you pay is listed before you begin.

The name reflects how we want to be reachable: consultation requests are accepted online at any hour, and a coordinator answers during opening times.

The medical team

Four roles share responsibility for your care. Doctors decide and perform medical procedures.

  • MD

    Medical director

    Dermatology doctor responsible for clinical protocols, treatment plans and safety review.

  • AP

    Aesthetic physicians

    Doctors who assess patients and perform injections, laser and energy-based procedures.

  • ST

    Skin therapists

    Trained staff delivering cleansing, peels and barrier care under a doctor's plan.

  • PC

    Patient coordinators

    Your contact for bookings, plans, reminders and follow-up visits.

Doctor names, photographs and practising licence numbers will be published here as supplied by the clinic.

Facilities

F.01

Private consultation rooms

Quiet rooms with skin imaging and good daylight, so assessments are made on what we actually see.

F.02

Treatment rooms

Separate rooms for laser, injectables and skin care, each set up for a single patient at a time.

F.03

Sterilisation area

Instruments are cleaned and sterilised in a dedicated area, apart from patient rooms.

F.04

Recovery area

A calm space to rest after a procedure while staff check your skin and go through aftercare.

How you are received

From the front door to your follow-up, five fixed points on every first visit.

  1. 01

    Check-in

    The coordinator confirms your details and gives you a short health questionnaire.

  2. 02

    Medical history

    We record allergies, medication, previous treatments and anything that affects safety.

  3. 03

    Doctor's assessment

    A doctor examines your skin, takes reference photos with your consent and discusses options.

  4. 04

    Plan and consent

    You receive the written plan and cost. Consent is signed only for the treatment you choose.

  5. 05

    Follow-up

    We book a review visit and send aftercare instructions you can keep.

Standards we work to

Six rules applied on every visit, whatever the service.

  1. 01

    Doctor-led procedures

    Medical procedures are performed or directly supervised by a licensed doctor.

  2. 02

    Written consent

    Risks, alternatives and aftercare are explained and signed before treatment.

  3. 03

    Traceable products

    Product name and batch number are recorded in your file for every injectable.

  4. 04

    Hygiene protocol

    Single-use consumables where possible and sterilised instruments for each patient.

  5. 05

    Clear records

    Settings, products and photos are kept so every follow-up starts from facts.

  6. 06

    No pressure to buy

    You can leave with your plan and decide later. We do not offer same-day discounts.
