Our medical direction

We treat the skin as an organ first and a surface second. That shapes how we assess, treat and price.

  1. 01

    Diagnosis before treatment

    A doctor reviews your skin, history and current medication before any procedure is suggested.

  2. 02

    Established protocols

    We use recognised techniques with documented settings, products and aftercare for each session.

  3. 03

    Plain pricing

    Reference prices are published. Your written plan lists every session and its cost before you start.

Three groups of care

Full list and reference prices
  • 01 Skin treatment Acne and acne scarring, pigmentation, redness and enlarged pores, treated after a medical skin assessment. 4 services
  • 02 Intensive skin care Medical-grade cleansing, peels and hydration programmes that restore the skin barrier between treatments. 3 services
  • 03 Non-surgical aesthetics Injectables, skin boosters and energy-based tightening, performed by doctors with no incisions. 3 services

How a consultation works

  1. 01

    Request

    Send the consultation form or call us. A coordinator confirms a time with you.

  2. 02

    Skin assessment

    A doctor examines your skin, asks about your history and explains what is realistic.

  3. 03

    Written plan

    You receive a plan with sessions, expected downtime and full cost. No decision is needed on the day.

  4. 04

    Treatment and review

    Treatment follows the plan, with a scheduled review to check results and aftercare.
