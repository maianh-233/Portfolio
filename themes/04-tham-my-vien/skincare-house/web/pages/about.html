“We wanted a place where you can ask every silly question.”

Dear future regular,

We met at a big dermatology clinic. Doctors had five minutes per patient and therapists had cream sales targets. Everyone left with more questions than they came with.

In 2021 we rented a townhouse on Ho Tung Mau, painted it in the colours we loved as kids, and wrote a few house rules: check first, say the price out loud, never sell at the bed.

Good skin does not need drama. It needs time, patience and someone who explains things.

Bao Ngoc & Minh Thu

Co-founders, Saigon
