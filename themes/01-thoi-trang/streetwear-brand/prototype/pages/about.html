Two friends, one rented room, our own rules.

The Mission began with a few design students who left school to print tees for the street. Seven years later, the spirit hasn't changed.

Real people, no retouching

From 30 hand-printed tees to a queue around the block.

  1. 2019

    Drop 001

    30 hand screen-printed tees made in a District 4 rented room. Sold out to friends in a single evening.

  2. 2021

    Closing the online store

    Resellers bought up stock and sold it at three times the price. We closed the online shop and now sell only in person at pop-ups.

  3. 2023

    The bridge pop-up

    A pop-up under Khanh Hoi Bridge, announced three hours ahead. 600 people came and everything sold out in 17 minutes.

  4. 2026

    Ton That Dam studio

    Our own print workshop and a team of 9, still no restocks. No Signal is our 11th drop.

Every piece passes through human hands.

No heat transfers, no mass factories. Every layer of ink is pulled by hand, so no two pieces are exactly alike. A small print flaw? Consider it a signature.

Four rules we always keep.

Black. White. That's it.

No third colour. Stand out through cut and attitude.

No restocks.

Each design is made exactly once. Missed it? Catch the next drop.

Real people as models.

Skaters, tattoo artists, rappers. No professional models, no retouching.

No advertising.

No paid ads, no influencer deals. People wear it because they like it.

“When I wear The Mission on stage, I don't need a stylist. The clothes speak for me.”

Dat Lun, rapper with Hem 9, model for No Signal
