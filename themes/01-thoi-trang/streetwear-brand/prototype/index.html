The Mission

No online store/ Once it's gone, it's gone/ Saigon since 2019/ Dress for yourself/ No restocks/ The street is our runway/

Made for people who dress for the street, not for approval.

The Mission started in a 12m² rented room in District 4, with two old sewing machines and a homemade screen-printing frame. Every tee was printed by hand, tested on the street and repaired when it tore.

Each collection tells a story about Saigon after midnight: sidewalks, skateboards, concrete and spray-painted walls. Black and white only. No third colour.
