Started by two former dancers who missed feeling pretty at the gym.

Linh and Vy trained in ballet for twelve years, then traded the studio for office jobs and spin classes. In 2023 they began sewing the pieces they wanted: soft colours, wrap details, fabric that holds you in like a leotard.
