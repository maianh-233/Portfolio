The brand

Gear for riders who dress the part.

MOTION DISTRICT started in a small Saigon workshop where friends swapped parts, painted helmets and planned night rides. We still design the same way: by riding first and drawing second.

Every piece has to work in traffic, look good off the bike and leave room for you to make it your own. No uniform, no rulebook.

"Freedom is a full tank and nowhere you have to be."

Ridden first

Street-ready style

Yours to change
