Starline 3/4 Helmet

A three-quarter shell with a hand-sprayed star, cut for short city runs and long night loops.

The design story

Inspired by

Old race numbers on bar-shop walls, and the way paint fades on a bike that lives outside.

Starline began as a sketch on a café napkin: a helmet that looks like it already has a few stories in it. We kept the shell compact, so it sits close to the head and does not shout on a small scooter.

The star is masked and sprayed by hand, then sanded back in places before the clear coat. No two shells wear their scuffs the same way.

Why it earns a place on the bike

  • 01

    Close-fitting shell

    A smaller outline that suits scooters and slim café racers.

  • 02

    Hand-finished graphic

    Every star is masked, sprayed and aged by hand.

  • 03

    Washable comfort liner

    Pads unclip in seconds for a rinse after hot days.
