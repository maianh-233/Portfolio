(02) About

Design is an argument, not a decoration.

Every project starts with a sentence, not a moodboard. If I can't write down what a brand needs to say in one line, no typeface or colour will rescue it. Once that line exists, the grid, the type and the images all have a job.

I work in a Swiss tradition: clear hierarchy, honest grids, generous white space. Then I break one rule on purpose, because a layout that never surprises is a layout nobody remembers.

“Reduce until it hurts, then add back the one thing that makes it yours.”
  1. 01

    Listen

    Interviews, audits and a one-line brief we both sign off before anything is drawn.

  2. 02

    Reduce

    Pencil first. Dozens of rough marks, cut down to three directions worth building.

  3. 03

    Compose

    Grid, type scale and image rules tested on real formats: a poster, a spread, a phone screen.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Kerning, colour proofs and a guideline your team can actually use without me in the room.
